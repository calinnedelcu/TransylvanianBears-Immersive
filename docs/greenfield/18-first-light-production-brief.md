# Production brief: First Light to Gate

Status: approved implementation target

## Purpose

The opening must establish the team, the world and the interaction contract before
the visitor reaches Project Nexus. It is not a loader, logo reveal or atmospheric
holding screen.

The visitor should understand within the first viewport:

- the name Transylvanian Bears;
- that this is a team building software, games, ML and research;
- that the world can be followed or bypassed through the Work index;
- that the architectural system reacts to their progress.

## Visual thesis

The opening is a contemporary inhabited fortification at blue hour. The structure is
derived from three Transylvanian principles, not copied from a monument:

- Prejmer: a strong inhabited ring around a visible common center;
- Viscri: mineral plaster, dark timber, human scale and imperfect adaptation;
- Biertan: successive thresholds that produce narrative depth.

The result is secular and contemporary. It must not read as Dracula, gothic fantasy,
steampunk, a sci-fi portal or a generic luxury castle.

## Hero composition

### Desktop

- The camera begins outside and above the inhabited ring, looking across the gate
  toward the common workshop core.
- The wordmark occupies the left/lower field and never covers the gate silhouette.
- The outer wall creates a deep foreground edge; the core remains visible through
  the gate as a promise of the next state.
- Mountains and sky provide depth but remain quieter than the architecture.

### Mobile

- A separate camera starts lower and closer to the gate.
- The title uses a maximum of four lines and stays above the lower interaction area.
- The workshop core, gate pivot and signal share one vertical axis.
- Lateral camera travel is removed; depth comes from forward motion and layer scale.

## Camera beats

| Beat | Meaning | Camera | World change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | this is a place | slow 3/4 aerial drift | first light reveals inhabited wall |
| Recognition | this is Transylvanian Bears | settle into hero frame | mark appears as negative space in gate |
| Signal | the system receives a problem | descend toward path | topographic signal crosses terrain |
| Response | six contributions react | align with gate pivot | six architectural lights answer in sequence |
| Gate | identity becomes apparatus | controlled push-in | six blades unlock and become a lens |
| Passage | the website becomes a world | pass through aperture | mineral space resolves into synthetic field |

All beats are reversible. No cut, video seek or random simulation owns the transition.

## Material system

- limestone: warm gray base, high roughness, subtle normal breakup;
- plaster: lighter and softer, never pure white;
- timber: near-black brown with restrained anisotropic highlight;
- oxidized brass: only pivot, track and six response points;
- smoked glass: aperture center and later Lens continuity;
- signal: cyan-white core with a soft physical halo, never a neon grid;
- vermilion: one decision point, not ambient decoration.

## Lighting arc

- Opening: cool sky, low warm occupancy lights and readable shadow detail.
- Signal: local cyan response, no global color wash.
- Gate: brass edges catch a narrow moving highlight.
- Passage: exterior light compresses behind the camera; the Lens becomes the key.

Atmospheric haze establishes scale. Bloom is limited to signal and aperture edges.
Film grain is subtle and screen-space stable.

## Interaction

- Native scroll controls the authored timeline.
- Pointer movement adds at most two degrees of local camera parallax.
- The signal responds gently to pointer proximity but does not become a cursor toy.
- A single audio button enables wind, distant workshop resonance and the gate cue.
- Open Work Index remains visible and fully functional throughout the sequence.

## Required production assets

- outer ring kit: straight wall, curved wall, inhabited bay, gate bay and two towers;
- workshop core silhouette with six response anchors;
- gate apparatus with six blades and stable pivot;
- terrain mesh with signal spline and path anchors;
- three mountain depth layers or one low-poly horizon set;
- mineral, plaster, timber, brass and glass PBR materials;
- desktop and mobile static posters exported from approved frames;
- optional ambient sky plate without architecture or text.

## Budget

- first visual response before the 3D chunk loads;
- opening animated payload target below 2.5 MB compressed;
- active textures below 96 MB estimated desktop and 48 MB mobile;
- fewer than 70 draw calls desktop and 40 mobile;
- fewer than 300k visible triangles desktop and 100k mobile;
- DPR 1-1.5 during motion;
- no long task above 100 ms during chapter activation.

## Acceptance

The opening is accepted only if a cold viewer describes it as a specific digital place,
not a logo animation over abstract geometry, and can still open Work immediately.
