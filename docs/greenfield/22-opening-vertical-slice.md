# Opening vertical slice / chapters 01-04

## Production target

The first 60-90 seconds must prove the complete experience language before later chapters receive final assets. It is a website with a cinematic world, not a game with mandatory objectives: semantic copy, direct anchors and the work index remain available while the world responds to scroll and optional pointer input.

## Identity rule

The seven-segment heraldic lock is the only primary emblem. It must always preserve:

- seven material voussoirs forming an open Gothic arch;
- the fortified bear and open-gate negative space;
- the central garnet pivot;
- a physical role in the world, never a decorative duplicate.

At the threshold it is a lock integrated into the gate. During departure the voussoirs become infrastructure. In Nexus the same seven-part grammar becomes the evidence shutter. No second central crest, wheel or generic shield is introduced in the same frame; guardian figures stay subordinate architectural details.

## Visual constraints

- The citadel occupies the environment; copy never masks the gate or seal.
- One dominant visual system and one dominant copy block are allowed per beat.
- Transitions originate in an existing object: seal to corridor, aperture to source plane, source plane to proof.
- Authentic project captures are mounted on physical apertures, monitors, glass or documents. They are not floating cards.
- Materials must carry different histories: aged brass, oxidized copper, walnut, wet limestone, smoked glass, graphite and parchment.
- Cyan is an instrumentation signal, garnet is the identity pivot, and warm light belongs to inhabited architecture. None becomes a full-page palette.
- Mobile receives a composed camera and horizontal chapter rail; it is not a cropped desktop scene.

## Performance contract

- WebGL remains the production renderer; no WebGPU migration is required for this slice.
- The opening seal stays within a restrained draw-call budget and reuses sector geometry.
- The first-light citadel is streamed as material-joined desktop and geometry-reduced mobile Meshopt GLBs.
- Environment lighting is generated once; dynamic lights are reserved for the gate response and user signal.
- Reduced motion removes the canvas mechanics, collectibles and quality switching while preserving the complete editorial sequence.

## Authentic asset replacements

The current dimensions and physical mounting are final enough to continue production. Replace only media content when originals arrive:

- clean Nexus RGB, segmentation and detection exports from the same camera frame;
- a short AirSim/UE5 flight capture without editor chrome;
- the validated real-world detection frame at source resolution and aligned to the synthetic camera when possible;
- project credits, exact team roles and verified paper/result links.

Until the matched Nexus triplet exists, the interface must state that the current authentic captures are not pixel-aligned comparisons.
