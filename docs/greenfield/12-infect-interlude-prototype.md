# Infect.exe / 1-bit breach

## Primary sources

- Project and browser build: https://shieldsentinel.itch.io/infectexe
- Official jam submission and results: https://itch.io/jam/1-bit-jam-7/rate/4068020
- 1-BIT JAM 7 rules: https://itch.io/jam/1-bit-jam-7
- Canvas behavior and fallback: https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/canvas.html
- Pixelated image rendering: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-3/#the-image-rendering

## Verified facts

- The jam theme was `You're the enemy` and entries had to use two colors.
- Infect.exe is a released HTML5 action game built with Unity.
- The player is a virus that hacks the GPU, breaches the SSD, disables firewalls and
  infects the CPU.
- The public controls are WASD, `E` to interact and click to shoot after unlocking.
- The official result is #19 overall, #8 theme and #14 art out of 56 entries.
- The public authors are ShieldSentinel, andreiChe and calin.nedelcu.

## Prototype loop

1. The amber light from The Buried Hands contracts into one square pixel.
2. The pixel expands into the authentic two-color language of Infect.exe.
3. The visitor selects GPU, SSD and CPU. Selection changes the authentic frame and
   rewrites the canvas signal; it is not a fabricated replica of the game.
4. Pointer movement perturbs nearby bits. Keyboard and touch use the same subsystem
   buttons and retain the complete meaning.
5. The official rankings remain visible in DOM, next to links to the build and result.
6. At the exit, the bits stop behaving like a game surface and align as observations
   for Research Crossing.

## Constraints

- The interlude stays short and cannot block the next chapter.
- No health, XP, score, collectible or fake terminal.
- No sound starts from scroll.
- Canvas has equivalent semantic text and controls outside the bitmap.
- Reduced motion renders a stable frame without scan animation.
