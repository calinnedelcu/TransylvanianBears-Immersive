# Vertical Slice 05-07 Art Bible

## Purpose

Chapters 05-07 are one authored passage from Project Nexus into Aegis and then SchoolMate. They are not a hub, a replay loop, or a branching game. The visitor performs one meaningful scan, sees the physical consequence, and continues through the same canonical route.

## Core image

The final Nexus detection frame thickens into a brass-and-blackened-steel school gate set inside the citadel. A student presents a phone. The scan sends a cyan signal through a visible five-stage mechanism, opens the turnstile, warms the corridor lights, and releases the camera into a lived-in school interior. The signal then becomes a SchoolMate request sent directly from a student device to the secretariat, one of the two recipient routes supported by the product.

## Continuity rule

`detection rectangle -> gate frame -> scanner beam -> transaction conduit -> corridor wayfinding -> SchoolMate request thread`

No cut returns the visitor to a hub. Every transformation preserves one recognisable line, frame, or pulse from the previous chapter.

## Material metamorphosis

- **05 Passage:** Nexus cyan glass and detection lines acquire thickness, bevels, bolts, and oxidised brass.
- **06 Access:** dark steel, smoked glass, rubber, brass contacts, ceramic tile, and a concentrated cyan scan light.
- **07 SchoolMate:** the same brass line becomes warm oak trim, paper, frosted glass, chalk, textile, and daylight-grey plaster.

The palette must not read as a generic sci-fi school. Technology is inserted into an old Transylvanian civic interior: pointed arches, deep stone reveals, carved bear heraldry, worn thresholds, timber doors, and contemporary access hardware.

## Architectural language

- One pointed stone arch inherited from the citadel frames the school entrance.
- A physical bear crest is embedded above the scanner, never rendered as a floating HUD.
- The corridor has asymmetry and signs of use: coat hooks, notice rails, radiator pipes, scuffed tile borders, books, papers, and indirect room light.
- The classroom and secretariat are glimpsed as connected spaces, not separate dioramas.
- Screens belong to believable objects: phone, gate terminal, classroom display, secretariat monitor.

## Camera grammar

- **05:** controlled lateral-to-forward convergence; the last Nexus frame remains in view until it becomes the gate.
- **06:** shoulder-height approach behind and beside the student, then a short hold on the phone/scanner relationship. The camera never becomes first-person gameplay.
- **07:** pass through the opened gate, skim the corridor, then settle into a wide editorial composition where the classroom and secretariat can be read together.
- Camera motion is spline-authored and reversible under scroll. The scan animation itself is local and deterministic.
- Desktop and mobile use separate authored curves. Mobile favors foreground occlusion and portrait depth instead of shrinking the desktop framing.

## Interaction contract

The only primary interaction is `Scaneaza accesul`.

1. Before interaction, the student waits with the phone near the reader.
2. Activating the control runs the canonical valid-token transaction.
3. Five physical stages illuminate in order: issue, present, gate role, atomic redeem, audit.
4. The turnstile opens and the corridor lighting changes from guarded cyan to inhabited warm-white.
5. The experience continues identically for every visitor.

`Expired` and `already used` may appear later as passive proof rows. They never change the route, ending, collected state, or required interaction count. Scrolling past the chapter reconstructs the canonical allowed state so navigation is never blocked.

## Project separation

Aegis and SchoolMate share a school context, not a product identity.

- **Aegis:** access token, server validation, atomic redeem, roles, audit.
- **SchoolMate:** announcements, requests, schedules, communication, administrative roles.

Their scenes connect spatially, but naming, evidence, copy, screens, and metrics remain distinct.

## Lighting

- Passage: cold moon fill with a narrow brass rim.
- Access: scanner cyan as the local key, low warm spill beyond the gate.
- SchoolMate: soft institutional daylight plus practical tungsten pockets.
- The opened gate must visibly alter the world, not merely an icon: practicals turn on sequentially and reveal occupied detail deeper in the corridor.

## Quality tiers

- **Cinematic:** authored GLB, selective shadows on hero props, animated signal, dust and subtle bloom.
- **Composed:** same geometry, composition, and interaction with reduced particles, render scale, and shadow budget.
- **Editorial:** real project media and the same linear explanation without WebGL dependence.

## Non-goals

- No role selection.
- No alternate endings.
- No personalised route.
- No inventory, score, achievements, or replay reward.
- No fake login or fake operational data presented as authentic evidence.
- No floating card wall inside the 3D scene.

## Definition of done

- The scene reads as a Transylvanian school passage before any copy is read.
- The scan visibly changes at least three physical elements: conduit, gate, and corridor light.
- Aegis and SchoolMate remain unambiguously separate projects.
- Direct navigation to chapters 05, 06, or 07 produces a coherent canonical state.
- Reverse scroll reconstructs every state without stale timers.
- The transition to chapter 08 begins from a real architectural opening, not an empty 3D void.
