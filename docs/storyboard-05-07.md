# Storyboard 05-07: The Inhabited Gate

## Sequence length

The sequence occupies chapters 05-07 and approximately one fifth of the full journey. Scroll controls camera and large-scale transformation. The scan control drives one short local event without blocking scrolling.

## Keyframes

### 05.1 / Detection residue

The Project Nexus detection plane remains close to camera. Its cyan corners cast real reflections on dark stone. The school entrance is only a silhouette behind it.

### 05.2 / Frame gains mass

The rectangle extrudes. Cyan edges become inset glass channels inside oxidised brass. Stone jambs close around the new object.

### 05.3 / Identity reveal

The camera clears the frame and reveals a pointed arch, the physical bear crest, the Aegis reader, and a student approaching from frame left. The gate is visibly part of the citadel.

### 05.4 / Phone preparation

The student raises the phone. The Aegis interface is shown on the device, perspective-correct and integrated into its glass. No floating screenshot appears.

### 06.1 / Scan invitation

Camera settles at shoulder height with phone and reader in one strong diagonal. The DOM command aligns with the reader rather than covering the scene.

### 06.2 / Token issued

On activation, the phone surface tightens to a high-contrast token state. The first brass contact lights. A restrained spatial cue starts the transaction.

### 06.3 / Presented and role checked

A narrow scan plane crosses the phone. Light travels from the reader into two visible mechanical chambers marked `PRESENT` and `GATE ROLE`.

### 06.4 / Atomic redeem

Two opposing contacts close around a single cyan core. The one-way nature is communicated physically: the rear contact locks after the pulse passes.

### 06.5 / Audit and consequence

The final cylinder stamps one audit line on the gate terminal. The turnstile rotates open, the crest catches warm light, and corridor practicals wake in depth.

### 06.6 / Crossing the threshold

The student passes first. Camera follows through the opened arc; the transaction conduit continues along the floor edge as a brass inlay.

### 07.1 / Corridor becomes system map

The brass inlay branches toward classroom, homeroom teacher, and secretariat. Signs and doors make the available roles spatial rather than presenting four UI cards.

### 07.2 / Classroom request

Inside the classroom glimpse, one student request addressed directly to the secretariat appears on a believable personal device. A paper-like light packet leaves the room along the inlay. SchoolMate also supports a direct homeroom-teacher route, but this authored passage follows only the secretariat route.

### 07.3 / Administrative passage

The packet passes an announcement rail and schedule board. The environment shows SchoolMate's scope without pretending Aegis and SchoolMate are the same build.

### 07.4 / Secretariat resolution

The packet arrives on the secretariat monitor. Its status changes to approved or rejected, the room light shifts subtly, and the result returns to the student's history. One verified workflow has changed the space.

### 07.5 / Editorial clearing

The camera settles into a legible architectural wide. Real Aegis and SchoolMate captures occupy the phone, gate terminal, classroom display, and secretariat monitor. The DOM layer supplies evidence, metrics, credits, and source links with generous contrast.

### 07.6 / Descent seed

Behind the secretariat, a stone service stair descends. The warm paper packet loses saturation and becomes the mercury-white guide line for chapter 08. No empty hold remains between worlds.

## Scroll and event ownership

- Scroll owns keyframes 05.1-06.1 and 06.6-07.6.
- The scan event owns 06.2-06.5.
- If the visitor scrolls beyond the scan hold, the director resolves the scan to `allowed` and opens the gate.
- Reverse scroll before the interaction zone restores the waiting state.
- Reduced motion resolves each transformation as a short crossfade with identical information.

## Editorial proof after the scene

The chapter 07 clearing shows:

- Aegis facts: 20-second TTL, short-lived token, server-side validation, atomic single-use redeem, role check, audit event.
- Passive result matrix: `ALLOW`, `EXPIRED`, `ALREADY_USED`.
- SchoolMate facts: announcements, requests/approvals, schedules, role-specific operations.
- Separate repositories, links, credits, and awards.

The proof layer is a website surface. It should not imitate a game HUD.
