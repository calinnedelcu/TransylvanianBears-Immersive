## 1. Creative North Star

### 1.1 Narrative promise

The visitor crosses one inhabited Transylvanian fortification as it changes function:

`PLACE -> PROBLEM -> INSPECTION -> PROOF`

| Chapter | Visitor question | Visual answer | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 / Threshold | Who made this, and what kind of work is it? | One inhabited Carpathian neo-Gothic citadel, physically marked by Transylvanian Bears and shared by six students and seven systems | The castle gate becomes an observation aperture |
| 02 / Synthetic field | What problem are we entering? | A synthetic aerial environment assembles as structured training material | A real project image separates from the world |
| 03 / Lens knot | What can I do here? | One scene can be inspected as raw image, segmentation, or detection | The selected reading becomes an evidence frame |
| 04 / Evidence | Is the claim verifiable? | Authentic media, method, scale, authors, and source link occupy a native editorial surface | A detection boundary extends toward Chapter 05 |

The emotional arc is `recognition -> curiosity -> agency -> confidence`. Spectacle may
support that arc but may not replace it.

### 1.2 Iconic central image

**The iconic image is the Carpathian aperture threshold.** At blue hour, the camera
stands before the pointed gate of an inhabited neo-Gothic mountain citadel. A physical
Transylvanian Bears crest is fixed above the entry; two abstract stone guardian bears
anchor its base; the portcullis and six structural blades are beginning to lift and
open. A small authored flight of bats crosses the moonlit upper negative space. Through
the gate, the visitor can already see the Project Nexus synthetic field. One cyan
signal crosses the worn path and terminates at one vermilion pivot. The team name sits
in the quiet left field; crest, gate, common core, and synthetic street share one depth
axis.

This image must communicate five things without explanatory narration, in this order:

1. `Transylvanian Bears` is the subject.
2. This is a Transylvanian/Carpathian neo-Gothic castle, not generic fantasy scenery.
3. The physical crest and guardian forms make the bear identity part of the place.
4. The architecture is an instrument that opens onto a real technical project.
5. One continuous system connects folklore, identity, interaction, and proof.

It is not a collage, a detached logo slate, dashboard chrome pasted onto architecture,
or a circular sci-fi portal. Every styleframe for Chapters 01-04 must be traceable back
to this image.

### 1.3 The four control sentences

- **Carpathian castle, not theme park.** Pointed masonry, steep roofs, inhabited bays,
  a working portcullis, and mountain weather establish a neo-Gothic Transylvanian
  citadel without Halloween dressing or ruin spectacle.
- **Instrument, not steampunk or neon.** Mechanisms expose a simple pivot, track,
  blade, portcullis, or frame. Cyan indicates active data, never decorative machinery,
  a global wash, or cyberpunk atmosphere.
- **Bear identity, not mascot.** A physical crest, compact mark, gate negative space,
  and abstract guardian-bear sculptures are required identity layers. Avoid fur,
  naturalistic anatomy, expressive faces, glowing eyes, or aggressive esports posing.
- **Folklore, not costume.** Sparse authored bat silhouettes, moon-cut shadows, and a
  restrained Dracula-derived threshold mood are valid. Literal Dracula characters,
  vampire costumes, coffins, fangs, blood effects, and jump-scare staging are not.

## 2. Visual Grammar

### 2.1 Governing rules

| Rule | Production implication |
| --- | --- |
| Form carries function | A shape may transform only when its role changes: gate to aperture, street frame to detection box, detection box to evidence frame |
| One world persists | Camera, horizon, light direction, and conserved geometry maintain continuity; portals and fade-to-black are fallback only |
| Evidence stays literal | Project screenshots, outputs, event photos, figures, and video are never regenerated, beautified into fiction, or presented without source context |
| Atmosphere yields to proof | Chapters 01-03 may be cinematic; Chapter 04 becomes brighter, quieter, flatter, and easier to read |
| Geometry precedes effects | Silhouette, depth, and material contrast must read with bloom, fog, grain, and particles disabled |
| Mysterious and occupied | Warm windows, visible work infrastructure, one or two controlled flame practicals, and moonlit depth imply use; sparse bats and abstract folklore cues may add tension, but abandonment and horror staging may not |
| Local asymmetry, global order | The ring and route are legible, while bays, roofs, wear, and landscape respond imperfectly to use and terrain |
| Web UI remains native | Titles, controls, metrics, captions, authors, links, and statuses remain semantic DOM, never texture-baked primary content |
| Interaction is optional to progress | Lens and proof controls deepen understanding but never block scroll, route access, or the evidence CTA |
| Reverse is authored | Every state has a deterministic predecessor. Reverse scroll reconstructs, rather than dissolves into, the previous frame |

### 2.2 Cardinality is semantic

Counts are not decoration. They must remain correct in geometry, animation, copy, and
sound.

| Count | Meaning | Allowed representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1` | One team, one citadel, one common system | Common core, route, signal, pivot |
| `6` | Six team members / contributors | Six response lights, six aperture blades, six workshop anchors |
| `7` | Seven shipped project systems | Seven inhabited bays, evidence sockets, or route marks; never seven aperture blades |
| `3` | Three Lens readings | Raw, Segmentation, Detection |
| `16` | Full guided journey | Persistent chapter rail only; do not imply sixteen projects |

The `6` and `7` systems must never be conflated. If all seven project marks cannot be
counted clearly in a shot, they remain latent in the architecture rather than appearing
as arbitrary repeated ornament.

### 2.4 Continuity chain

| From | To | Conserved element | Required transformation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Threshold path | Gate | Cyan signal terminus | The path reaches the physical pivot; it does not teleport upward |
| Gate | Synthetic field | Six-blade opening and central axis | Mineral blades leave frame as street boundaries enter on the same vectors |

Every major transition conserves at least one silhouette, vector, material edge, or
function. A full-screen opacity fade may hide a loading failure, but it is not an
approved authored transition.

### 2.5 No-go list

- Halloween-attraction Transylvania, generic fantasy-kit castles, ruin spectacle,
  overlaid dark-academia props, or neo-Gothic ornament without structural purpose;
- literal Dracula/vampire characters, actors, costumes, coffins, fangs, blood effects,
  jump scares, monster reveals, or explanatory folklore copy;
- cartoon or stock bat silhouettes, dense swarms, random perpetual flight, bat screeches,
  or bats crossing the title, crest, gate opening, chapter rail, or controls;
- generic royal heraldry, crowns, weapons, unrelated shields, glowing bear eyes,
  naturalistic/taxidermic bears, furry mascots, or aggressive esports branding;
- portcullis, crest, guardian-bear, torch, or gargoyle motifs repeated as decorative
  wallpaper instead of one authored gate composition;
- steampunk gears, pipes, gauges, brass filigree, alchemy props, or mechanical clutter;
- circular portal, wormhole, eye icon, brain, neural-network web, or hologram;
- decorative code, terminal rain, fake charts, fake model outputs, or unsourced
  coordinates;
- permanent chromatic aberration, glitch, heavy bloom, crushed black, fog used to hide
  unfinished work, or camera shake;
- floating cards over the world, nested cards, trophy shelves, or dashboard chrome;
- generated text, generated logos, or generated media that can be mistaken for project
  evidence;
- animation that captures wheel/touch input, mandatory hover, autoplay audio, or motion
  that continues after the visitor stops.

## 3. Architecture and World Design

### 3.1 Cultural synthesis

The opening must read as a Carpathian neo-Gothic castle while remaining an original
Transylvanian Bears place. It combines structural principles rather than copying one
recognizable monument:

| Source principle | Translation |
| --- | --- |
| Prejmer | A strong inhabited ring surrounds a visible common center |
| Viscri | Human scale, mineral plaster, dark timber, simple stone, practical openings |
| Biertan | Successive thresholds create depth and narrative progression |
| Carpathian neo-Gothic | Pointed entry, steep controlled roofs, vertical stone ribs, iron gatework, and moon-cut silhouette |
| Transylvanian folklore | Physical bear heraldry, paired guardians, sparse bats, and threshold shadows used as authored identity cues |

The world is secular, inhabited, and contemporary in function even when its silhouette
is historical. Do not reproduce sacred interiors, religious symbols, a known castle's
landmark silhouette, or a tourist-view composition.

### 3.2 Massing rules

- The common center is visible or directionally implied in every Threshold master shot.
- The gate is ceremonial enough to announce the team and broad enough to feel used. Its
  scale may be monumental in the first frame, but human doors, wear, and occupied bays
  must keep it believable.
- Wall bays repeat at a readable rhythm but vary in roofline, wear, window state, and
  depth by no more than one structural module.
- Towers are compact neo-Gothic orientation points. Steep roofs and a few pointed
  silhouettes are valid; repeated needle spires, stacked turrets, and skyline clutter
  are not.
- The approach path bends once and then resolves to the gate axis. It may not form a
  glowing circuit-board landscape.
- Architecture frames the media transition. It may not remain as a decorative border
  once Project Nexus becomes the subject.
- A physical Transylvanian Bears crest is mandatory above or within the gate. The
  negative-space bear remains a secondary discovery and the compact UI mark remains its
  small-scale derivative.
- Paired guardian bears frame the threshold as low-poly/faceted stone masses or reliefs.
  They remain subordinate to the gate and never animate as creatures.
- A working iron portcullis is a required depth and transition layer. It lifts on the
  gate axis before the aperture opens; it is not a weapon close-up or repeated motif.
- One sparse bat flight is authored into the opening: `3-9` visible silhouettes at peak
  on desktop and `2-5` on mobile. It stays in upper negative space and clears the title,
  crest, opening, and controls.
- One or two flame practicals may support the gate hierarchy when warm windows are
  insufficient. They are functional fixtures, not a symmetrical torch corridor.

### 3.3 Folklore and Dracula grammar

Dracula is a tonal and graphic reference, never an on-screen character or claim about
the team. Use at least two of these cues in `KF-01`/`KF-02`, and no more than four in one
frame:

- a moon-cut pointed shadow that briefly suggests a high collar or wing profile;
- a sparse, art-directed bat flight with readable spacing and a single direction;
- matte oxblood recessed in the physical crest, paired with aged brass or black iron;
- a portcullis shadow crossing the threshold like a measured graphic cadence;
- a steep roof or pointed arch held against cold sky, balanced by occupied warm light.

The cues must survive as silhouette and material decisions with post effects disabled.
They may not become lore exposition, literal vampire iconography, an Easter-egg hunt,
or evidence about the projects.

### 3.4 Synthetic-field rules

The synthetic field is an authored bridge into Project Nexus, not a claim that the
procedural website city is one of the project's Unreal Engine environments.

- Use the procedural field for spatial continuity, scenario variety, and interaction
  only.
- Introduce authentic `ue5-industrial-aerial.png` before making any factual claim about
  the project's generated environment.
- Keep proxy buildings simple enough to read as a data model, not a parallel game
  environment built for the website.
- Use ordinary streets, occupied windows, survey paths, and visible terrain context;
  avoid cyber-city spectacle.
- Segmentation and detection colors change semantic surfaces only. They do not recolor
  the entire sky, light rig, or architecture.
- Drone motion communicates capture. It is not a hero vehicle and receives no close-up
  product shot.

## 4. Color System

### 4.1 Core palette

| Token | Value | Role | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Night mineral | `#070A0B` | World background, deepest shadow, dark UI | Never the only visible surface value |
| Ink | `#111718` | Dark text on paper, secondary world structure | Maintain separation from Night mineral |
| Limestone | `#E4E1D9` | Primary light text, plaster highlight, active controls | Do not use as pure-white glow |
| Paper plaster | `#C9C4B9` | Evidence clearing | Chapter 04 and explicit proof moments only |
| Quiet metal | `#909A98` | Secondary copy, inactive labels | Body text still requires contrast pass |
| Signal cyan | `#72D9D6` | Active route, Raw state, focus, verified continuation | Maximum about 8% of a frame outside media |
| Oxidized brass | `#BFA96F` | Pivot track, structural join, Segmentation state | Mechanisms only; never a luxury-gold wash |
| Crest oxblood | `#491318` | Matte recessed field in the physical crest; folklore undertone | Maximum about 3% of the opening; never emissive, wet, or used as blood |
| Vermilion | `#DF6553` | One decision point, Detection state, current selection | Maximum one dominant pivot per frame |
| Moss | `#60735C` | Landscape, living secondary contrast | Never used as a digital status color |

Authentic project media keeps its source colors. Grade the surrounding world and UI
down before altering evidence. A source image may receive a documented display-only
exposure/contrast correction, but no content-aware replacement, generative fill, or
semantic recoloring.

### 4.2 Chapter color arc

| Chapter | Dominant relation | Accent behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 / Threshold | Cool moon/sky + warm occupied apertures + neutral mineral + matte oxblood crest | Cyan is one path; vermilion is one gate pivot; crest red remains dark and non-emissive |
| 02 / Synthetic field | Desaturated mineral city + authentic source media | Cyan reveals structure; brass marks class grouping sparingly |
| 03 / Lens knot | World remains neutral while mode color changes locally | Raw = cyan, Segmentation = brass, Detection = vermilion |
| 04 / Evidence | Paper plaster + ink + dark evidence viewport | Cyan indicates source/verified links; media carries the richest color |

### 4.3 Contrast and color acceptance

- Normal text meets WCAG `4.5:1`; large text and essential icon boundaries meet `3:1`.
- Focus indicators meet `3:1` against adjacent colors and are at least `2px` thick.
- Selected, verified, pending, and inactive states never rely on hue alone.
- Evidence labels remain legible over the darkest and lightest parts of the media.
- Black detail remains visible on a calibrated sRGB display at 20% brightness; no
  essential geometry may live only below approximately `#161A1A`.
- Color-blind simulation must preserve mode selection through icon, label, and boundary
  pattern, not merely cyan/brass/vermilion.

## 5. Material System

### 5.1 PBR targets

Values are look-development ranges, not automatic shader constants.

| Material | Base behavior | Roughness | Metalness | Detail rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limestone | Warm gray, porous, edge wear from use | `0.82-0.96` | `0.00` | Broad tonal breakup; no noisy micro-rock at hero distance |
| Mineral plaster | Lighter, soft, irregular application | `0.86-0.98` | `0.00` | Subtle normal and variation between bays; never pure white |
| Blackened timber | Near-black brown, visible grain direction | `0.68-0.88` | `0.00-0.05` | Restraint in highlights; no plastic or theme-park finish |
| Oxidized brass | Darkened body, polished only at contact edges | `0.32-0.48` | `0.65-0.82` | Pivot, track, and six response points only |
| Crest relief | Matte oxblood recess with aged brass/black-iron bear relief | `0.38-0.72` | `0.35-0.78` | Physical depth reads in moon edge; no internal glow, jewel sparkle, or stock shield decal |
| Guardian stone | Dark local stone, broad planes, softened exposed edges | `0.88-0.98` | `0.00` | Faceted bear mass reads at medium distance; no fur normal, wet nose, eye shader, or fine anatomy |
| Smoked glass | Neutral charcoal transmission | perceptual `0.12-0.28` | `0.00` | Aperture center and lens only; no holographic tint |
| Worn path | Dense stone with one directional wear band | `0.90-0.98` | `0.00` | Signal must sit physically on/in the path, not float above it |
| Evidence display | Color-managed, minimally reflective | N/A | N/A | sRGB source, correct aspect ratio, no fake glare or scanline unless explained |
| Signal emitter | White-cyan core with restrained halo | emissive | N/A | Local light contribution only; no global emissive wash |

### 5.2 Material continuity

- The same limestone, plaster, timber, and brass families persist through Chapters
  01-03. Project media changes; the world's material identity does not reset.
- The gate's brass track becomes the Lens track. Its UV direction and wear orientation
  remain continuous through the transformation.
- Detection frames are graphic, one-pixel-to-two-pixel screen-space lines. They are not
  extruded glowing tubes.
- Chapter 04 shifts from PBR depth to paper tactility. A narrow mineral edge may bridge
  the transition, but the clearing must read as a native web surface.
- Primary evidence is displayed with `sRGB` color interpretation. Unlit media planes
  use `toneMapped={false}` only after visual comparison with the DOM fallback.

### 5.3 Texture and geometry discipline

- Model only surfaces seen by authored cameras. Hidden architectural detail is out of
  scope.
- Repeated bays, windows, response anchors, and street furniture use instancing or
  batching.
- Hero mineral texture sets may use up to `2048px` desktop and `1024px` mobile when
  measured; repeated trim should use shared or tiled textures.
- No baked text, chapter numbers, captions, metrics, or controls in 3D textures.
- Normal maps may break highlights but may not destroy the clean silhouette at mobile
  resolution.
- Evidence media preserves source aspect ratio. Crops require a documented focal point
  and a separate mobile crop decision.

## 6. Lighting, Atmosphere, and Post

### 6.1 Lighting hierarchy

1. **Readable subject light:** gate, signal terminus, field, or evidence plane.
2. **Identity light:** moon edge across the pointed gate, crest relief, and guardian
   silhouettes without a heraldic spotlight.
3. **Occupied practical light:** restrained warm windows, work points, and no more than
   two controlled flame fixtures in the opening composition.
4. **Environmental fill:** cool blue-hour sky with detail in mineral shadows.
5. **State accent:** cyan, brass, or vermilion at the current operation only.
6. **Atmosphere:** depth haze and sparse stable grain after the frame already reads.

### 6.2 Chapter light arc

| Chapter | Key | Fill | Practical / accent | Exposure intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Cool high soft sky and moon edge from camera-left | Open blue-hour horizon with readable mountain separation | Warm occupied bays, up to two flame practicals, matte crest, local cyan path, one red pivot | Reveal an inhabited neo-Gothic castle and bear identity before technical activation |
| 02 | Neutral-cool overhead survey light | Low-contrast ground bounce | Cyan capture beam and sparse brass joins | Expose street form and data state equally |
| 03 | Same field rig; no theatrical relight per mode | Lens glass remains nearly neutral | Mode color affects reticle and semantic surfaces only | Preserve comparison between states |
| 04 | Broad paper daylight with soft vertical falloff | Neutral white/gray | Dark evidence viewport and cyan verified links | Reading and source inspection take priority |

### 6.3 Post-processing limits

- Use one ACES Filmic pipeline. Current runtime exposure `0.78` is the calibration
  baseline, not a license to underlight assets.
- Bloom is selective and may not exceed the current cinematic baseline (`0.42`
  intensity, `0.82` threshold) without shot review.
- Grain is screen-space stable, subtle, and removed from evidence media when it impairs
  inspection.
- Vignette may guide the opening, but it may not darken copy, navigation, the Lens
  control, or evidence edges.
- Depth haze separates planes; it may not obscure the common center or synthetic-field
  subject.
- No chromatic aberration or glitch in Chapters 01-04.
- Ambient particles are sparse, slow, and disabled in composed/editorial tiers.
- Bats are authored silhouette objects, not particles or post effects. Their bodies stay
  opaque enough to read against sky, and they receive no bloom, motion-trail, or depth-
  haze treatment that turns the flight into a swarm.

## 7. Camera and Motion Language

### 7.1 Camera contract

- The camera travels on an authored rail. It never free-orbits the citadel or asks for
  FPS navigation.
- Forward travel is the dominant movement. Lateral offsets exist only to reveal
  parallax or keep UI clear of the subject.
- Desktop starts at approximately `48deg` vertical FOV. Mobile starts at approximately
  `57deg` to retain the gate and vertical route; neither FOV pumps for spectacle.
- Pointer parallax is local, optional, and below `2deg`. It is disabled for reduced
  motion and touch.
- Velocity roll remains below `1deg` and returns to zero at rest.
- Horizon remains stable. No Dutch angles, whip pans, handheld shake, rack-focus blur,
  or collision camera.
- Camera and look target are authored independently; the visitor should look through
  the gate before moving through it.
- Every camera state is deterministic from normalized progress. Time-driven ambient
  motion may not alter framing or transition endpoints.

### 7.2 Shot grammar

| Shot | Use | Composition rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establishing 3/4 | Threshold arrival | Ring, gate, center, and one landscape layer visible; brand has a quiet field |
| Axial approach | Signal and gate | Gate pivot remains within central 20% of viewport; no orbit |
| Survey descent | Synthetic field | Camera looks down enough to explain capture but retains horizon/context briefly |
| Operational hold | Lens | Camera settles; pointer moves the Lens, not the whole world |
| Editorial release | Evidence | Camera motion ends before dense reading begins; DOM surface owns the frame |

### 7.3 Motion timing and scroll behavior

- Native scroll is the only progression input. Do not intercept wheel, touch, arrows,
  Page Up/Down, Home, or End.
- Authored transforms use monotonic easing and reversible progress. Spring overshoot is
  allowed only on nonessential micro-feedback, never camera or evidence.
- When scroll stops, camera and structural transformation settle within `250ms`;
  ambient wind or subtle occupancy may continue only when motion is allowed.
- Fast travel must resolve directly to the destination chapter's stable state. No
  unloaded flash, half-open gate, missing evidence plane, or stale mode label.
- Cross-chapter transition zones reserve roughly the final/first `12-18%` of each
  chapter-local timeline. Exact scroll length is calibrated after the animatic.
- Current implementation distances (`185dvh`, `170dvh`, `185dvh`, `190dvh`) are
  prototype baselines, not locked duration.
- The bat flight has one authored departure across `KF-01 -> KF-02`, never loops, and
  is never driven by pointer chasing. Reduced motion uses a static composed silhouette
  set in the poster; reverse scroll restores the deterministic prior placement.

### 7.4 Reduced motion

The reduced-motion route is a complete editorial version, not a frozen WebGL scene.

- Use five stable states across the slice: exterior, open threshold, synthetic field,
  Lens comparison, evidence clearing.
- Replace travel, parallax, aperture rotation, scanning loops, and scrubbing with an
  instant swap or a crossfade no longer than `200ms` when allowed.
- Put all content in normal document flow; sticky behavior is optional and may not
  create relative parallax.
- Preserve mode controls, metrics, authors, source links, Work Index, chapter navigation,
  and audio preference.
- Stop decorative animation, particles, drone movement, scanline loops, and velocity
  audio modulation.

  position, and returns focus to the invoking control on close.

### 8.4 In-scene UI

- At most one chapter title, one supporting statement, and three facts appear over a
  complex moving scene.
- Copy sits in authored negative space, not a translucent floating card.
- A dark directional grade may protect text but may not cover more than half the frame
  as an opaque panel.
- Lens controls are a segmented tool: icon + visible label, one selected state, and
  `aria-pressed` semantics. Desktop descriptions may be present; mobile labels remain.
- Pointer inspection is enhancement only. Touch and keyboard use explicit mode buttons.
- Project metrics, captions, authors, and links are selectable semantic text.
- The proof inspector must state that explanatory overlays are interpretive unless the
  exact original model output is being shown.
- Coordinates, confidence values, class names, and award wording appear only when
  sourced. Current demo coordinates and synthetic confidence labels are placeholders.

### 8.5 Language

The current launch copy is Romanian with established English technical labels. Art
layouts must survive both Romanian and English without changing composition logic.

- Test Romanian diacritics in every weight.
- Reserve at least `30%` expansion for translated body copy and controls.
- Do not embed copy in posters, videos, textures, or generated images.
- Keep project names and technical standards in their official form.

### 8.6 Nexus agency system

Nexus uses bounded local agency inside an otherwise authored cinematic route. These
interactions are production behavior; their current geometry remains subject to art lock.

| Probe | Production disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `nexusFlightInputRef` pointer/WASD/Arrow drone steering | FINAL behavior baseline. Steering is clamped to the survey envelope and never alters the authored camera. Keyboard input activates only while the Lens surface owns focus. |
| `EvidenceCoreField` proximity pickups and `evidenceCores` collection state | FINAL behavior baseline. Source, Structure, and Decision are optional spatial pickups that persist across modes; they never gate proof, routes, source access, or chapter completion. |

Failure to use either mechanic must produce the same chapter exit, evidence, routes, and
facts. Public UI may use `flight`, `collect`, and `core` for this local interaction, but
never `mission complete` or `unlock` because progression remains unconditional.

## 10. Sound Direction

Audio is off by default and starts only after the existing explicit control is used.
It supports material and transition; it never carries unique information.

### 10.1 Sonic palette

The authored controller uses four continuous stems and three explicit spatial cue
families. Additional cues remain placeholders until they are added to that contract.

| Runtime stem / cue | Character | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `citadel` | Filtered Carpathian wind and low occupied-stone resonance | Dominant at first light, then remains a quiet spatial anchor |
| `threshold` | Gate friction, low mechanical body, slow amplitude drift | Rises through Chapter 01 and clears as the field resolves |
| `nexus` | Moving HRTF drone, three restrained harmonics, filtered data air | Crossfades through Chapters 02-03 and follows Lens mode |
| `evidence` | Sparse air and stable low partials | Enters late in Chapter 03 and remains the quietest proof layer |
| `threshold-open` | Six restrained contacts resolving into one soft lock | Explicit, latched aperture-commit event; count must remain six |
| `lens-lock` | One family retuned for Raw, Segmentation, or Detection | Explicit Lens selection only |
| `evidence-reveal` | Neutral paper/glass contact | Explicit, latched proof-panel availability event |
| Future handoff cue | One boundary impulse toward Chapter 05 | PLACEHOLDER until added to the sound controller |

### 10.2 Mix and behavior

- Master true peak is at or below `-1 dBTP`.
- Ambient bed targets roughly `-30` to `-24 LUFS` short-term; cues may rise no more than
  `6 LU` above the bed.
- The vertical-slice controller fades in over `450ms`; mute completes within `80ms`.
- Scroll velocity may change filter brightness and texture density. It may not change
  pitch, pan aggressively, or add more than `3dB` apparent loudness.
- Stopping scroll stabilizes reactive sound within `250ms`.
- Audio adaptation receives progress normalized over Chapters 01-04. Do not pass the
  full 16-chapter global journey progress directly.
- Spatial cues fire from explicit, latched state/selection events, never from raw
  progress sampling and never repeatedly around a sentinel.
- Mode cues fire only from explicit pointer, touch, or keyboard activation.
- Menu open, hidden document, and page blur suspend reactive modulation.
- Reduced motion disables velocity response but does not force mute.
- No copyrighted project sound is used without recorded rights and credit.
- Folklore remains visual. Do not use bat screeches, wolf howls, pipe organ, thunder
  hits, whispered voices, heartbeat, vampire stings, or jump-scare transients. One
  restrained wing-air pass may accompany the authored bat departure only after audio
  opt-in and must sit below the gate/occupancy bed.

## 11. Asset Status Ledger

### 11.1 Status definition

- **FINAL:** approved to ship for the stated role. A derivative may still need web
  optimization, but its factual or visual content is locked.
- **PLACEHOLDER:** useful for blockout, interaction, layout, or source reference, but it
  must be replaced, corrected, or explicitly relabeled before art acceptance.

Status is role-scoped. A compound status such as `FINAL design foundation /
PLACEHOLDER delivery` locks the concept and its required presence while identifying the
current mesh, material, motion, font package, or poster as unfinished. Refinement may
replace that delivery; it may not silently remove or reverse the approved foundation.

No asset is silently promoted from placeholder because it appears in the current
production route.

The runtime registry in `verticalSliceAssets.ts` uses a more granular delivery stage.
For art approval, it maps to the binary status required here:

| Runtime stage | Art-bible status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `placeholder` | PLACEHOLDER | Slot or content is blocked/incomplete |
| `proxy` | PLACEHOLDER | Working geometry/camera/poster, not visual final |
| `candidate` | PLACEHOLDER | Reviewable delivery that has not passed all gates |
| `source-approved` | FINAL source / PLACEHOLDER delivery | Factual source is approved; derivative remains placeholder until the stable URL exists and passes crop, color, rights, and QA |
| `final` | FINAL | Stable delivery exists and all visual, evidence, responsive, and runtime gates pass |

## 12. Production and Delivery Rules

### 12.1 Runtime ownership

- One persistent R3F canvas owns the continuous world in Chapters 01-03.
- Chapter 04 is primarily DOM and responsive media; WebGL may carry the transition edge
  but may not delay the clearing.
- Current and adjacent chapters may prepare heavy resources. Distant chapter assets do
  not remain active.
- Scene modules expose deterministic `prepare`, `activate`, `update`, `deactivate`,
  `dispose`, and `fallback` behavior.
- React/XState owns meaningful discrete state: chapter, quality, motion, audio, and Lens
  mode. Render-loop refs own continuous progress and velocity.

### 12.2 File and export rules

- Keep source originals unchanged and version web derivatives separately.
- Use stable names for gate blades, pivot, response anchors, media anchors, and camera
  targets.
- Export one GLB package per chapter ownership boundary; validate with Khronos glTF
  Validator and optimize with glTF Transform.
- Compare Meshopt and Draco rather than assuming one compression path.
- Record compressed bytes, visible triangles, draw calls, and estimated texture memory
  for desktop and mobile exports.
- Generate desktop and mobile posters from approved cameras, not unrelated paintings.
- Every evidence derivative records original path, crop, color treatment, caption,
  credit, rights state, and where it appears.

### 12.3 Performance budgets

| Budget | Desktop target | Mobile target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First visual response | Poster and identity before 3D chunk | Same, with dedicated vertical poster |
| Critical poster | `<=360 KiB` | `<=220 KiB` |
| Initial realtime bytes | `<=2.5 MiB` | `<=1.25 MiB` |
| Total slice transfer | `<=14 MiB` | `<=7.5 MiB` |
| Active texture memory | `<96 MB` estimated | `<48 MB` estimated |
| Peak total GPU memory | `<=128 MiB` estimated | `<=64 MiB` estimated |
| Visible triangles, opening | `<300k` | `<100k` |
| Draw calls, opening | `<70` | `<40` |
| Resident chapter packages | Maximum `2` | Maximum `2` |
| Device pixel ratio during motion | `1.0-1.5` | `1.0` |
| Frame rate | Stable `60fps` reference | Stable `30fps` minimum, `60fps` preferred |
| Chapter activation long task | No task `>100ms` | No task `>80ms` |

Quality may fall during a chapter. It may rise only at the next chapter boundary.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The slice is art-approved only when every criterion below passes. “Looks good” is not
an approval state.

### 13.1 Narrative and identity

- **AB-01:** In an unprompted five-second test, at least four of five first-time viewers
  identify `Transylvanian Bears` as a team that builds software, games, ML, and research.
- **AB-02:** At least four of five describe the opening as a Transylvanian or Carpathian
  neo-Gothic castle/citadel associated with bears. No more than one describes it as a
  Halloween attraction, generic vampire game, literal Dracula tableau, theme-park set,
  game menu, or detached logo animation.
- **AB-03:** The iconic aperture-threshold frame preserves the required read order:
  Transylvanian castle -> bear identity/crest -> instrument -> Project Nexus -> proof.
- **AB-04:** Six member responses and seven project systems are never visually or
  verbally conflated.

### 13.2 Visual language

- **AB-05:** Gate, aperture, Lens, and evidence frame visibly share geometry in forward
  and reverse motion without a dominant fade or portal effect.
- **AB-06:** The frame reads with bloom, particles, grain, and fog disabled.
- **AB-07:** Cyan remains functional and local; vermilion has one dominant decision
  point per frame; evidence media remains the richest source of color.
- **AB-08:** `KF-01`/`KF-02` include the required structural neo-Gothic gate, physical
  crest, abstract guardian bears, working portcullis, and authored bat flight. At peak,
  bats number `3-9` on desktop and `2-5` on mobile, do not obscure UI or identity, and
  never read as a swarm; no literal Dracula, Halloween, mascot, steampunk, cyberpunk,
  or fake-science treatment remains.
- **AB-09:** The physical crest reads as original Transylvanian Bears heraldry at its
  authored camera distance; the compact derivative reads as bear/ring/gate at `16`,
  `24`, `32`, and `64px` in monochrome and blur-silhouette tests. Guardian bears remain
  recognizable as faceted stone forms without naturalistic faces, eyes, fur, or motion.

### 13.3 Camera, motion, and transition

- **AB-10:** Camera endpoints match within visual tolerance after slow scroll, fast
  scroll, scrollbar drag, Home/End, and reverse traversal.
- **AB-11:** Reverse scroll reconstructs every prior state without a jump, one-frame
  asset flash, orientation flip, or stale DOM label.
- **AB-12:** Pointer parallax stays below `2deg`, velocity roll below `1deg`, and both
  return to rest within `250ms`.
- **AB-13:** No authored motion captures native scroll or continues as required motion
  after scrolling stops.

### 13.4 Evidence and truth

- **AB-14:** A first-time viewer can explain within `8-12s` that Project Nexus uses
  synthetic data and is tested on real aerial data.
- **AB-15:** `11 scenarios`, `~9,500 images`, and `>140,000 automatic annotations`
  appear with units, source context, and no invented accuracy claim.
- **AB-16:** Authentic source media is visually distinguishable from atmospheric proxy
  geometry and explanatory overlays.
- **AB-17:** Every confidence value, coordinate, author, result, and source label is
  verified or removed. Explanatory diagrams are explicitly labeled.
- **AB-18:** The source CTA is a real link, works without WebGL, and does not wait for a
  transition before navigation.

### 13.5 UI, access, and responsive behavior

- **AB-19:** All primary copy, controls, metrics, captions, authors, and links are
  semantic DOM and remain available with canvas disabled.
- **AB-20:** Keyboard order follows visual order; every mode is operable with keyboard
  and touch; focus is visible and never clipped.
- **AB-21:** Essential text meets the defined minimum sizes and WCAG contrast; controls
  provide at least `44 x 44px` targets.
- **AB-22:** At `1440 x 900`, `1024 x 768`, `390 x 844`, and `360 x 800`, there is no
  overlap among header, rail, title, subject, controls, metrics, or safe-area insets.
- **AB-23:** Mobile uses its own camera, poster, focal points, and layout; no desktop
  frame is accepted through cropping alone.
- **AB-24:** Reduced motion and Tier C preserve all entities, facts, modes, routes, and
  actions in normal reading order without required parallax, scan, or scrub.

### 13.6 Sound and performance

- **AB-25:** Audio remains off until explicit activation, mute is immediate, and no
  chapter cue repeats around a sentinel.
- **AB-26:** Lens cues match explicit selections and contain no exclusive information;
  reactive sound stabilizes within `250ms` after scroll stops, and the opening contains
  no bat screech, wolf howl, organ, thunder hit, whisper, heartbeat, or vampire sting.
- **AB-27:** The measured payload, texture memory, triangle, draw-call, DPR, frame-rate,
  and long-task budgets in Section 12.3 pass on reference devices.
- **AB-28:** No console error, WebGL warning, missing asset, layout shift from late font
  load, or audio lifecycle leak occurs during a complete forward/reverse traversal.

### 13.7 Asset completion

- **AB-29:** Every ledger item used in the shipping slice is marked FINAL. The required
  Carpathian castle, physical crest, guardian bears, portcullis, and authored bat flight
  cannot pass by omission: each PLACEHOLDER delivery is refined or replaced while its
  FINAL design role remains. Other placeholders are replaced, intentionally omitted,
  or visibly identified as non-documentary explanation.
- **AB-30:** Desktop and mobile posters, GLBs, media derivatives, captions, credits,
  rights status, and performance measurements are archived with reproducible sources.
- **AB-31:** Pointer/WASD/Arrow drone steering works inside a bounded, focus-scoped
  envelope, remains reversible, and never moves the authored camera. Proximity evidence
  pickups persist across modes but never gate progress, native navigation, or proof.

Passing these criteria approves Chapters 01-04 as a production vertical slice. It does
not automatically approve later chapters; they inherit this grammar and must pass their
own evidence, interaction, responsive, and performance gates.
